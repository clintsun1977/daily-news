Four soldiers die in fast-moving fire at CFB Wainwright艾伯塔省温赖特军事基地大火致4名军人死亡

A fast-moving fire tore through a training area at CFB Wainwright in Alberta on October 17, killing four service members and injuring 39 others, 19 of them critically. The dead are Sgt. Morgan Wong and Privates Shaun Farrell, Aidan Goulding and Hayden Lykkemark, all based at the Edmonton Garrison. Prime Minister Mark Carney told the House of Commons on Monday that the Canadian Forces National Investigation Service, supported by the RCMP, is leading a complete investigation, with fifty investigators on the ground. A National Defence official said there are no indications of terrorist activity. Winds of up to 69 kilometres an hour, and an uncommon shift in direction, helped the flames spread. General Jennie Carignan said the findings will be made public, and a board of inquiry and an Alberta coroner's investigation will follow. Flags are at half-mast on federal buildings. Early reports of seven deaths were corrected to four, and the original cause is still unknown.

10月17日，艾伯塔省温赖特加拿大军事基地在训练期间发生迅速蔓延的大火，造成4名军人死亡、39人受伤，其中19人伤势危重。遇难者为埃德蒙顿驻军的摩根·黄中士，以及肖恩·法雷尔、艾登·古尔丁和海登·莱克马克三名列兵。总理卡尼10月19日在下议院发表声明，说加拿大军队国家调查局正在皇家骑警协助下展开全面调查，目前已有50名调查员到场。国防部官员表示，没有迹象显示这起事件涉及任何恐怖活动，火势受大风推动，风速最高达每小时69公里，风向突然出现罕见转变。军方总参谋长卡里根说，调查结果将对外公布，同时还会成立调查委员会，艾伯塔省也将展开验尸官调查。联邦建筑物已降半旗志哀。早期报道曾称至少7人死亡，后经确认为4人，起火的最初原因目前仍不清楚。

Albertans vote about three to one to stay in Canada艾伯塔省公投：约四分之三选民选择留在加拿大

Albertans voted on Monday to reject a move toward separation, by roughly three to one. Al Jazeera reported about 77 per cent support for remaining in Canada and 23 per cent for starting a process, and CBC called the result after about a third of the vote was counted. The question was not a direct vote on independence; a yes would have set the province on a path toward a later binding referendum. Prime Minister Mark Carney said Albertans "made a clear choice" and "chose unity." Premier Danielle Smith said she was very pleased, but argued she still needs a yes on the nine other questions to win a mandate to bargain with Ottawa. Those cover immigration and the Constitution, including provincial selection of judges, abolishing the Senate and giving provincial laws priority in shared areas. Early returns on those nine were closer. Separatist leader Wilson said about one in four voters want a different relationship with the rest of Canada.

艾伯塔省10月19日举行公投，选民以约三比一的比例拒绝启动可能通向脱离加拿大的程序。半岛电视台报道，约77%的选民支持留在加拿大，约23%支持启动相关程序；加拿大广播公司在计票三分之一左右时就宣布了结果。这并不是直接的独立公投，而是问是否开启通往日后具有约束力的脱离公投的程序。总理卡尼说，“10月19日，艾伯塔人做出了明确的选择”，称他们选择了团结。省长史密斯表示对结果“非常高兴”，但她说自己需要在另外九个问题上得到“是”的答案，才能获得与渥太华谈判的授权，这九个问题涉及移民和宪法事务，包括由省政府任命省级法院法官、废除参议院，以及在共同管辖领域让省法律优先。早期开票显示，这九个问题上选民意见较为分歧。分离主义领袖威尔逊则说，约四分之一的投票者希望改变艾伯塔与加拿大其他地区的关系。

Canada's inflation rises to 3.1% as gasoline climbs加拿大9月通胀升至3.1%，汽油价格同比上涨27.8%

Statistics Canada reported on October 19 that the consumer price index rose 3.1 per cent from a year earlier in September, up from 3.0 per cent in August and slightly below the 3.2 per cent that economists polled by Reuters had expected. Energy was the main driver. Gasoline prices were up 27.8 per cent from a year ago, after a 22.8 per cent increase in August, and excluding gasoline the index rose 2.2 per cent. Transportation costs rose 8.4 per cent and shelter 1.8 per cent. Food bought in stores climbed just 1.7 per cent, the smallest annual increase since May 2024. The Bank of Canada's two preferred core measures eased, with CPI-median at 1.9 per cent and CPI-trim at 1.8 per cent. The central bank announces its next interest-rate decision on October 28, and this report will be part of that debate.

加拿大统计局10月19日公布，9月消费者物价指数同比上涨3.1%，高于8月的3.0%，略低于路透社调查中经济学家预期的3.2%。推高通胀的主要是能源：汽油价格同比上涨27.8%，8月的涨幅为22.8%；若剔除汽油，整体通胀为2.2%。交通类价格同比上涨8.4%，而超市食品价格仅上涨1.7%，是自2024年5月以来最小的年度涨幅，住房类上涨1.8%。加拿大央行关注的两项核心通胀指标有所放缓，其中CPI中位数为1.9%，CPI修剪均值为1.8%。加拿大央行将于10月28日公布下一次利率决定，这组数据将成为决策者的重要参考。

Back-to-back job losses leave the Bank of Canada divided连续两个月流失岗位，加拿大央行10月28日决策面临分歧

Statistics Canada reported earlier this month that the economy lost about 68,000 jobs in September, after a loss of roughly 41,700 in August, and the unemployment rate edged up from 6.4 to 6.5 per cent. Economists had expected a gain of about 9,200 jobs. Young workers took the biggest hit. Quebec lost 49,000 jobs and British Columbia 20,000, while Alberta added 23,000. A KPMG economist said it would be premature to blame tariffs for the weakness. With inflation now at 3.1 per cent, the Bank of Canada faces a tough call. Some reports say big-bank economists expect it to hold its rate at 2.25 per cent, while others say BMO, Royal Bank and Desjardins now expect a hike to 2.5 per cent, so views are split. The decision and the Monetary Policy Report are due on October 28.

加拿大统计局此前公布，9月就业岗位减少约6.8万个，8月也减少约4.2万个，失业率由6.4%升至6.5%。经济学家原本预测9月会增加约9200个岗位，结果出乎意料。青年就业人数降幅最大，魁北克减少4.9万个岗位，不列颠哥伦比亚省减少2万个，而艾伯塔省增加2.3万个。毕马威的一位经济学家表示，现在就把疲弱归咎于关税还为时过早。与此同时，9月通胀升至3.1%，让央行处境尴尬：有报道称，几家大银行的经济学家预期央行将维持2.25%的利率不变，也有报道称加拿大皇家银行、蒙特利尔银行和德雅尔丹预计将加息至2.5%，各方说法并不一致。央行将于10月28日上午公布利率决定和货币政策报告。

Pete Fry of the Green Party of Vancouver was elected mayor in the October 17 municipal election, becoming the city's first Green mayor and ousting incumbent Ken Sim. Preliminary results show Fry with more than 98,000 votes, about 31,000 ahead of Sim, on a turnout of 209,000 that was up 36 per cent from four years ago. On council, the Greens and the Coalition of Progressive Electors each won four of the ten seats, and OneCity Vancouver took a ninth, giving the left-of-centre camp a majority. The business-friendly ABC Party, which held seven seats after 2022, was reduced to one. Fry has served on council since 2018, and his mother is Liberal MP Hedy Fry. 10月17日的市政选举中，绿党的皮特·弗莱当选温哥华市长，成为该市首位绿党市长，现任市长肯·西姆败选。初步结果显示，弗莱得票超过9.8万张，领先西姆约3.1万张；投票人数约20.9万，比四年前增加36%。市议会十个席位中，绿党和进步联盟（COPE）各拿下四席，“温哥华一城”（OneCity）拿下第九席，左翼阵营占多数。亲商的ABC党在2022年曾拥有七个议席，这次只剩一席。弗莱自2018年起担任市议员，他的母亲是自由党联邦议员海迪·弗莱。据维基百科资料，他将于11月2日就任。

Ottawa moves to make defence procurement agency a Crown corporation国防采购署将改组为皇家公司，加拿大转向非美供应商

The Carney government has tabled legislation to turn the Defence Investment Agency into a Crown corporation, which would run at arm's length from government and report to a new associate minister of national defence for procurement. New analysis by the Parliamentary Budget Officer estimates Ottawa would have to spend $164.6 billion in 2035-36 to reach a target of five per cent of GDP on defence. Canada is also moving away from U.S. suppliers. CTV reported it is negotiating to buy Swedish Saab GlobalEye surveillance aircraft instead of an American competitor's system. CBC reported a U.S. contract notice showing Canada is included in a US$1.1-billion HIMARS production deal that Ottawa had not announced. In July, Carney named Thyssenkrupp Marine Systems the preferred supplier for the Canadian Patrol Submarine Project, which the government calls the largest defence procurement in the country's history, with contracts due by the end of 2027.

卡尼政府近期提交法案，把国防投资署改组为皇家公司，使其独立于联邦政府运作，并向新设的国防采购助理部长汇报。国会预算官的新分析显示，若要在2035至2036年度达到国防开支占GDP的5%的目标，联邦政府需要支出1646亿加元。加拿大还在摆脱对美国供应商的依赖：据报道，加拿大正与瑞典洽购萨博的“全球之眼”空中预警机，而不是美国的竞争产品；另据加拿大广播公司报道，美国的一份合同通知显示加拿大被列入一项价值11亿美元的海马斯火箭炮生产合同，渥太华此前并未公布。7月，卡尼已宣布蒂森克虏伯海事系统公司为加拿大巡逻潜艇项目的首选供应商，政府称这是加拿大历史上最大的国防采购，合同最迟将于2027年底前完成。

Ford pledges Ontario capital for Alberta-to-Sarnia Northern Shield pipeline福特表态愿出资，阿尔伯塔至萨尼亚“北方盾牌”输油管引争议

Ontario Premier Doug Ford said on September 28, at an energy summit in Calgary alongside Alberta Premier Danielle Smith, that his government is "ready to step up with the capital necessary to build the Northern Shield pipeline." The proposed line would run about 3,300 kilometres from Hardisty, Alberta, to Sarnia, Ontario, carrying roughly 500,000 barrels a day, with room to expand to 800,000. The premiers announced the plan on July 6. Ford also said Ontario will not pay for the pipeline alone and expects outside investors to join. The project is still at the feasibility stage, with no private proponent, no cost estimate and no formal submission to Ottawa. Critics ask why taxpayer money is on offer for a project of unknown cost. Supporters present it as an all-Canadian answer to the trade dispute with the United States. Ottawa is separately moving to designate a West Coast pipeline a project of national interest.

安大略省长福特9月28日在卡尔加里一场能源峰会上与艾伯塔省长史密斯同台，表示安省政府“准备好拿出建设北方盾牌输油管所需的资金”。这条拟建管道全长约3300公里，从艾伯塔省哈迪斯蒂通往安省萨尼亚，初期日输送约50万桶原油，可扩建至80万桶，两位省长7月6日宣布了计划。福特同时表示，安省不会独自承担成本，期待外部投资者加入。目前项目仍处于可行性研究阶段，没有私营开发商，也没有官方成本估算，批评者质疑，为何要为一个造价不明、尚无投资者的项目动用纳税人的钱。渥太华另外正在推动把西海岸输油管道认定为国家利益项目。支持者把北方盾牌视为应对美加贸易争端的全加拿大方案。

Peel Regional Police announced on October 19 the results of Project High Guard, an eight-month investigation by the Central Robbery Bureau into violent carjackings and high-end vehicle thefts across the Greater Toronto Area. Ten people were arrested and 128 charges laid in connection with 21 incidents involving about 40 victims. Police recovered 15 stolen vehicles worth about $1.4 million and seized four illegal firearms, along with ammunition and magazines. Eight men and two youths face charges, including two 17-year-olds from Mississauga and Brampton; the accused range in age from 19 to 37 and live in Oakville, Brampton, Mississauga and Halton Hills. The incidents ran from June 2025 to April 2026, and some involved knives or guns and serious injuries. Police describe a coordinated network whose members targeted vehicles, organized robberies and recruited younger offenders. The OPP and Toronto, Halton and Hamilton police assisted, and the investigation remains open.

皮尔区警方10月19日公布“高卫行动”（Project High Guard）的结果：劫案调查组经过约八个月调查，逮捕10人，共提出128项指控，涉及大多伦多地区21起暴力劫车和豪车盗窃案，受害者约40人。警方追回15辆被盗车辆，总值约140万加元，并缴获四支非法枪械及弹药。被捕者共八名男子和两名青少年，其中包括来自密西沙加和宾顿的两名17岁少年，年龄在19至37岁之间，居住在奥克维尔、宾顿、密西沙加和霍尔顿山等地。警方说，案发时间从2025年6月持续到2026年4月，部分案件使用刀具和枪支，有受害者被殴打受重伤。警方表示，这是一个有组织的犯罪网络，成员分工负责选车、策划抢劫、招募年轻人并从中牟利。安省警察厅提供情报支持，多伦多、霍尔顿和哈密尔顿警方协助执行搜查令，调查仍在进行。
