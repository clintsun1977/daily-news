Fire at CFB Wainwright in Alberta kills four service members阿尔伯塔温赖特军事基地大火致4名军人死亡

A fast-moving grass fire and explosion hit CFB Wainwright in Alberta on Oct. 17, killing four service members and injuring dozens, with at least 19 in critical condition. Prime Minister Mark Carney called it one of the worst tragedies for the Forces on Canadian soil in peacetime. A preliminary investigation points to a munition from a live training exercise igniting the fire, and National Defence says there is no sign of terrorism. Early reports of seven dead were later corrected to four by defence officials.

10月17日，阿尔伯塔省温赖特加拿大军事基地发生快速蔓延的草原大火并伴有爆炸，4名军人死亡，数十人受伤，其中至少19人情况危急。总理卡尼称这是加拿大军队在本土和平时期最严重的悲剧之一。初步调查显示，实弹训练中的弹药可能引燃了大火，国防部表示没有迹象显示与恐怖活动有关；早期曾有7人死亡的报道，后经国防官员确认为4人。

Alberta votes Monday on a path toward leaving Canada阿尔伯塔省今日就是否启动脱离加拿大程序公投

Albertans vote Monday, Oct. 19, in a referendum that includes a question on starting a legal process that could lead to independence, plus nine other questions on immigration and constitutional reform. Elections Alberta says more than 1.3 million people had already voted by mail or at advance polls. A CBC poll found 72 per cent would vote to remain in Canada and 23 per cent to start the process. Premier Danielle Smith says she voted to stay and has ruled out a second separation referendum if Albertans vote to remain.

阿尔伯塔省10月19日（周一）举行公投，其中一项问题涉及是否启动可能通向独立的法律程序，另有9项涉及移民和宪法改革的问题。选举部门称，投票日前已有超过130万人通过邮寄或提前投票投票。CBC民调显示72%受访者会选择留在加拿大，约23%支持启动程序；省长史密斯表示她投了留在加拿大，并且若公投结果是留下，她不会再办第二次分离公投。
