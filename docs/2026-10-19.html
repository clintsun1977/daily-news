Four soldiers killed in fast-moving fire at CFB Wainwright艾伯塔省温赖特军事基地大火致4名军人死亡

A fast-moving grass fire swept through CFB Wainwright in Alberta on October 17, killing four service members and wounding 39 others, 19 of them critically. The dead were Sgt. Morgan Wong and Privates Shaun Farrell, Aidan Goulding and Hayden Lykkemark, Edmonton-based soldiers of the 3rd Battalion, Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry, who were training for a deployment to Latvia. Maj.-Gen. Wade Rutland said a grass fire had been burning for some time but was thought to be far enough away and contained, so training went on. When a storm kicked up, he said, flames moved about a kilometre in roughly 30 seconds and overwhelmed the soldiers. Prime Minister Mark Carney told the House of Commons that Canada is "united in grief," and said fifty investigators are on the ground. Officials do not yet know how the fire started, and it could take weeks to find out. Early reports of seven deaths were later corrected to four.

10月17日，艾伯塔省温赖特加拿大军事基地发生一场迅速蔓延的草原大火，造成4名军人死亡、39人受伤，其中19人伤势危重。遇难者为埃德蒙顿第三营公主爱国轻步兵团的摩根·黄中士，以及肖恩·法雷尔、艾登·古尔丁和海登·莱克马克三名列兵，他们当时正在为部署拉脱维亚进行训练。少将韦德·拉特兰表示，一场草火已烧了一段时间，被认为距离较远且已受控，训练因此继续；风暴突起后，火焰在约30秒内推进了一公里，吞没了官兵。总理卡尼在下议院说，加拿大“同悲共痛”，并表示加拿大军队国家调查局正在皇家骑警协助下主导调查，已有50名调查员到场。官员称起火原因尚不清楚，可能需要数周才能查明，目前没有迹象显示涉及弹药或油气设施爆炸。早期报道曾称至少7人死亡，后经卡尼确认为4人。

Albertans vote on Monday, October 19, on whether to remain a province or start the legal process toward a future binding referendum on separation. A vote for the second option would not make Alberta independent right away. Premier Danielle Smith called the vote but opposes independence, and said on October 14 that she had voted against secession. Polls point to a lopsided result: an Abacus Data survey released Saturday found 68 per cent intend to stay in Canada, and an AP report cited a recent poll putting support for starting the process at around 23 per cent. Al Jazeera asked whether the movement can survive a loss.

艾伯塔省10月19日（周一）举行公投，选民要在留在加拿大与启动通向未来具有约束力的分离公投的法律程序之间做出选择。投赞成启动程序的一票并不意味着艾伯塔会立即独立。省长丹妮尔·史密斯召集了这次投票，但本人反对独立，并在10月14日表示自己投了反对票。民调显示分离一方处于劣势：Abacus Data周六公布的民调称68%的人打算投票留在加拿大，美联社援引的另一项民调则显示支持启动程序者约占23%。已有136万人通过邮寄和提前投票参与。分离派领袖基思·威尔逊的团体周六表示，即使公投失败也将继续推动独立。半岛电视台则在探讨分离派在落败之后能否继续存在。

Canada's inflation rate rises to 3.1% in September on fuel prices加拿大9月通胀升至3.1%，汽油价格推高

Statistics Canada reported on October 19 that consumer prices rose 3.1 per cent from a year earlier in September, up from 3.0 per cent in August. Gasoline was the main driver, with pump prices up 27.8 per cent over the year, compared with 22.8 per cent in August. Excluding gasoline, the index rose 2.2 per cent. Grocery prices slowed, with store-bought food up 1.7 per cent after 2.8 per cent in August. The Bank of Canada's preferred core measures were lower, at 1.9 per cent for CPI-median and 1.8 per cent for CPI-trim. Reuters had forecast 3.2 per cent, so the reading came in slightly below expectations. It was the last inflation report before the central bank's October 28 rate decision, with the policy rate at 2.25 per cent.

加拿大统计局10月19日公布，9月消费者价格指数同比上涨3.1%，高于8月的3.0%。汽油是主要推手：汽油价格同比上涨27.8%，8月为22.8%；不含汽油的CPI同比上涨2.2%。超市食品价格涨幅放缓至1.7%，8月为2.8%。CPI中位数为1.9%，CPI修剪均值为1.8%。路透此前预测的是3.2%，因此这一数字略低于市场预期。这是加拿大央行10月28日利率决议前的最后一份CPI数据，央行目前的政策利率为2.25%。

Manitoba declares measles outbreak over after 1,038 cases曼尼托巴宣布麻疹疫情结束，累计1038例

Manitoba has declared its measles outbreak over, effective October 16, after no new cases since August. The outbreak was first declared in February 2025. Officials counted 1,038 cases in total, 931 lab-confirmed and 107 probable. Fifty-two people were hospitalized, six of them in intensive care, and no deaths were reported. About 87 per cent of cases involved people who were unvaccinated or whose vaccination status was unknown. Early vaccine eligibility for infants, which had been expanded during the outbreak, has ended. The province warned that measles remains widespread in parts of the world and that outbreaks are continuing in the United States.

曼尼托巴省政府宣布麻疹疫情结束，自10月16日起生效。该省最后一例实验室确诊病例出现在8月，疫情最早于2025年2月宣布。官员统计累计1,038例，其中931例经实验室确诊、107例为疑似；52人住院，6人入住重症监护，没有麻疹死亡报告。约87%的病例是未接种疫苗或接种状况不明的人。疫情期间扩大的婴儿提前接种资格也已随之结束。政府同时警告，麻疹在世界部分地区仍然流行，美国的疫情也仍在持续。
