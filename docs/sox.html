Paper trading: 8 rules compared, $10,000 each模拟交易：8 种规则对比（各 $10,000 起始）
Unless a rule says otherwise: if the forecast is above the SOXX pre-market move at the call, buy SOXL (3x bull) at the open, otherwise SOXS (3x bear), whole shares with the whole balance, and sell at the close. Every trade pays 0.05% each way for fees and slippage. Thresholds are measured on the SOX index.除非规则另有说明：预测涨幅高于预测时的 SOXX 盘前涨幅就按开盘价买入 SOXL（三倍做多），否则买入 SOXS（三倍做空），用全部余额买整数股，收盘价卖出。每次买入和卖出各扣 0.05% 的手续费和滑点。各规则的触发线都以 SOX 指数衡量。
Rule details规则说明
  • A Sell at the close.收盘价卖出。
  • B Sell as soon as the SOX's move on the day reaches twice the forecast, either way.SOX 当日涨跌幅一旦达到预测值的两倍就卖出（止盈或止损）。
  • 1 Baseline that ignores the forecast: SOXL if the pre-market is up, SOXS if down.基准对照，不看预测：盘前涨买 SOXL，盘前跌买 SOXS。
  • 2 Size by the forecast's confidence: high 100%, medium 50%, low 25% of the balance; the rest stays in cash.按预测信心定仓位：高 100%、中 50%、低 25%，其余留现金。
  • 3 Trade only if the forecast differs from the pre-market move by at least 0.5 points; otherwise stay in cash.预测与盘前涨跌相差至少 0.5 个百分点才交易，否则空仓。
  • 4 Sell once the SOX moves one daily standard deviation (last 20 days) from its open, either way.SOX 相对开盘价涨跌达到 1 倍日波动率（近 20 天）就卖出。
  • 5 Once the SOX is up 1% from the open in our favour, sell if half of the best gain is given back.SOX 相对开盘价朝有利方向走出 1% 后启动保护，回吐一半最大涨幅就卖出。
  • 6 Wait until 10:00: buy then only if the SOX has moved our way since the open; otherwise no trade.等到 10:00，SOX 相对开盘价朝预测方向走才买入，否则当天不交易。
